# The City Chronicles: interface direction (v4, "the City at night, seen through glass")

The City fills the screen and changes with the life being lived: a Fixer walks an amber night, a Feather lives under the clean light of the Nest, the Index rains down Prescripts, a WARP car streaks past its windows. Every panel is dark smoked glass over that world, so the world is always present and the words stay legible. There is no light theme.

- Idea: the world is the background, and the background tells you where you are. Each route, a Hana Color, an awakened or open E.G.O, a dungeon and death each have their own sky, skyline, weather, accent and sound.
- `src/engine/sceneContext.js` (`sceneContextOf`) feeds the backdrop (`src/components/world/`), route identity and ambience (`src/engine/ambienceScapes.js`). Interface controls use the fixed gold tokens in `src/styles/ui/tokens.css`, including on the opening screen and archive pages.
- Typefaces: Literata for the story, headings and names; Archivo for the instruments, labels and figures (tabular numbers).
- Colour: ink ground (#07070a), bone text (#f1ede6), smoked glass background panels. Gold (#d8b265) identifies primary actions, selected controls, milestones, Ahn and Hana evaluations throughout the game. World art and earned nameplates retain their route colours. HP, SP and the other stats keep their own hues with written labels.
- Sound: ambience only, no music. It is made from noise and mechanical events: rain, wind, city rumble and distant sirens, office air and typing, the Index typewriter, WARP rails, a forge, crowds, the Great Lake tide, Carmen's whispers, the L Corp alarm, a heartbeat in danger.
- Motion: a change of world crossfades over about 1.6s, new chronicle entries rise, the decision dock rises, cards lift, a dungeon result shakes, and a Color or E.G.O gets one burst of light. Weather runs on one capped canvas. Everything stops under reduced motion.
- Shell: one top bar (brand, which also goes home; archive tabs; sound; a settings menu for language, text size and motion). On a phone the tabs move to a bottom bar.
- Play: the running head, the dossier column and the chronicle column (the darkest glass). A decision is an opaque card in the middle of the screen over a dimmed City, on every width. Dialogs share `--dialog-surface`, `--dialog-border` and `--dialog-scrim`; their text never shows the scene through it. Settings and NPC dialogs render through a portal outside the blurred bars and dossier panels.
- Year: a working year opens on one card: three time slots, the life's ambition the first time, and the three leads; picking a lead starts the year. A dungeon's door shows the risk for the current condition and a wager of 0–2 before entering.
- Dungeons: each place is an illustrated, moving stage that fills the screen (`DungeonBackdrop.jsx`, `dungeon-scenes.css`): a WARP car with the City streaking past, an L Corp containment corridor with a turning siren, the La Manchaland wheel under a red moon, Outskirts ruins in the heat, the Great Lake from below, a Backstreets alley at 3:13, the Claw's judgment room. While a dungeon card is open the chronicle steps aside; the scene remains visible around the opaque reading card. Its dungeon colour marks the card edge and route information.
- Nameplate: the top seat of each branch dresses its own way (`rankPlateOf` gives tier and route): a Hana Color gets a double frame and rays in the conferred colour, the Index a ruled Prescript slip in typewriter type, the Middle wide heavy type and a striped edge, the Ring paint and an italic signature, the Pinky a centred frame inside the frame, the Thumb condensed capitals and chevrons, the Wing board a corporate pass with a blue band. One step below gets a coloured rule.
- Rules: tokens only, no hard-coded colours; root tokens on `:root`, never `:root.dark` (it outranks media queries and the text-size setting); sentence-case labels; no gradient text; no emoji icons.
- Checks: 1440 and 390 wide, several worlds, keyboard focus, reduced motion. Screenshots go in the scratchpad, never in the repo.

This direction was written by the agent at the owner's request; the owner can change any line.
